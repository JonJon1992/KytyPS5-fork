Target is Linux, RADV, RX 9070 XT. Fork numbers come from Windows/NVIDIA/Wolverine and do not transfer.
Related worktree: `/home/jonathanbraga/kyty-bindless-wt` (bindless-cow, holds uncommitted KYTY_READBACK_SIDE_WRITES).

**Key distinction:** our "64 KiB = +32-44% in Crash 4" setting is the **write fault-ahead** window (`KYTY_FAULT_AHEAD_ADAPT=64`). Senaxx's 512 KiB / 4 MiB window is the **read download** window. On our side that is `KYTY_READBACK_SIDE_COPY_WINDOW_KB` (default 64 KiB, cap 1 MiB) plus a hard-coded 512 KiB window on the drain path.
